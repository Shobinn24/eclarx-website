Verified Reviews Audit

How many of your product reviews come from real buyers? TallySeal Reviews Audit compares every review against your store's actual order history and tells you — then lets you publish the answer as a storefront trust badge.

What it does

Audit from anywhere your reviews live

Upload a CSV export from any review platform, or connect Loox and Judge.me directly and audit without an export.

Matched against real orders

Every review is checked against your store's order history on three signals: buyer email, product purchased, and purchase timing. You get clear verdicts — matched, likely match, customer bought a different product, or no matching order found.

The rating gap

See whether verified buyers rate your products differently than reviewers with no matching order. It is often the most interesting number in the report.

The storefront badge

When you are happy with your result, publish it: an "audited reviews" badge on your storefront showing your verified match rate. Rendered by a theme app extension with zero impact on page speed — no scripts, no external calls.

Private by design

Only aggregate results are stored — never customer emails, never review text. Read the full privacy policy.
